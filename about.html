The Hat Maker

Made by hand.

A personal millinery practice built around shape, fit, material and the person who will wear the finished hat.

The work

Canadian Hat Maker is intentionally small and personal. The appeal of the original site is already there: the work is made by one maker, not presented as anonymous mass retail.

This upgraded site keeps that same voice. Felt, straw, custom commissions and restoration remain the focus, with appointments and direct conversation at the centre of the process.

Every hat begins with proportion: crown, brim, material, trim and fit. The goal is not simply to make a hat, but to make the right hat for its wearer.
